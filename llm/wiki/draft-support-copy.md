## Import help

Kinship imports a user-selected Instagram official data export on your device. Export formats can change, and unsupported or incomplete files may not import. Review the app's warnings and coverage information; a partial export does not establish that omitted accounts are absent. Kinship cannot determine whether an account renamed, deleted, blocked you, or exactly when a relationship changed.

## Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Kinship uses RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement verification. Use the in-app restore-purchase option if available. Purchase restoration depends on the store account and service availability. Do not send receipts or account credentials through an unverified channel; support contact and any secure process must be provided by the owner.

## Data and privacy

Imported history is stored locally in the app's SQLite database. iOS device/computer backup may include app data according to platform behavior and your settings. Purchase-related information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat; Instagram export contents and relationship lists are not sent to them by Kinship.
