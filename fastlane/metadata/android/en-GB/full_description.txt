Kinship helps you understand your Instagram connections using data from your official Instagram export.

Import an export file on your device to view your current follower and following lists. Review connection details without sharing your Instagram password.

Kinship does not log in to Instagram or upload your export. Results reflect information in the export you provide and may not represent changes made since it was created.

Your export is processed on your device. Kinship is an independent app and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
